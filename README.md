## Signature Work

The projects that best represent how I think and what I can do.

### [When Traffic Volume Hides Conversion Quality](https://github.com/joonhanofficial/sql-acquisition-funnel-analysis)
`SQL` `BigQuery` `GA4`

The highest-volume acquisition source was not the highest-value one. A session-level funnel analysis of GA4 ecommerce data showing why volume and conversion efficiency are different problems — and why optimising for one without the other creates a measurement blind spot.

### [When SaaS Activation Looks Stable Until You Segment It](https://github.com/joonhanofficial/saas-activation-diagnostic)
`Python` `pandas` `seaborn`

A 65/35 activation split looked acceptable at the aggregate level. Segmentation by referral source and plan tier revealed an 11-point gap that the top-line rate was hiding. A case study in why segment-level thinking changes the interpretation of seemingly stable metrics.

### [Mapping Olist Brazilian Ecommerce Dependence](https://github.com/joonhanofficial/olist-marketplace-dependence-analysis)
`Power BI` `DAX`

Top-10 sellers accounted for 13.15% of total revenue. Top category share sat at 9.26%. A two-page Power BI dashboard examining whether seller and category concentration represented meaningful marketplace dependence — and what the distribution shape says about platform risk.

## All Case Studies

### SQL

**[When Traffic Volume Hides Conversion Quality](https://github.com/joonhanofficial/sql-acquisition-funnel-analysis)**  
The highest-volume acquisition channel produced the weakest downstream conversion efficiency. A session-level funnel analysis separating volume signal from quality signal across organic, direct, referral, and paid traffic.

**[Stability Over Speed: Post-COVID Recovery](https://github.com/joonhanofficial/stability-over-speed-post-covid-recovery)**  
Multi-country SQL analysis comparing GDP growth trajectories against inflation pressure across five economies — testing whether recovery speed was a reliable proxy for recovery quality.

### Python

**[When SaaS Activation Looks Stable Until You Segment It](https://github.com/joonhanofficial/saas-activation-diagnostic)**  
An aggregate activation rate masked an 11-point performance gap between acquisition and plan tier segments. A segmentation-led diagnostic showing how the interesting signal sits one level below the headline metric.

### Power BI

**[Mapping Olist Brazilian Ecommerce Dependence](https://github.com/joonhanofficial/olist-marketplace-dependence-analysis)**  
Seller, category, and regional concentration analysis across 96,000+ orders — examining whether the marketplace's revenue structure represented meaningful dependence risk or normal distribution spread.

### Strategy and Behavioral Analysis

**[Behavioral Audit: Spotify Wrapped vs Apple Replay](https://github.com/joonhanofficial/spotify-wrapped-vs-apple-replay-behavioral-growth-audit)**  
Two products with similar features, different distribution outcomes. A behavioral growth analysis examining how product design and sharing mechanics shape organic visibility — relevant for understanding growth loops and distribution strategy.
